When the core goes

Every profession plotted against the year its core tasks are expected to be routinely machine-done. Not the year the job title disappears — the difference matters.

As of August 2026, 15 of the 17 professions on willaistealit.com carry a time horizon — the year by which their core tasks are expected to be routinely machine-done. Those horizons run from 2028 to 2035, and 2030 is the most crowded year with 4 professions. The remaining 2 are judged safe and carry no horizon at all.
